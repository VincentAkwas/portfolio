About

Laboratory results shape clinical decisions, yet most people never learn to read them. I’m a fourth-year MLS student and self-taught developer working to close that gap with tools that are free, fast and built for the people who need them.

My work covers lab-result interpretation, case-based learning, psychometric assessments, research statistics and AI-driven automation. I’ve completed clinical placements at the Greater Accra Regional Hospital (Ridge) and Nyaho Medical Centre, and I write about laboratory science on my blog.

My aim is to make medical laboratory knowledge open to students, professionals and patients, one useful tool at a time.

Journey

Education, clinical training and research.

2026 – Present

Final-year project, Immunology and Serology

A group study, “Comparative Assessment of Widal Test Reactivity and Typhidot IgM/IgG Results Among Patients with Suspected Typhoid Fever at a Selected Hospital in Ghana”. It compares the two serological methods, measures how well they agree, and examines discordant results (Widal-positive/Typhidot-negative and the reverse) alongside age, duration of fever and previous antibiotic use.

Sept – Oct 2026

Industrial attachment, Nyaho Medical Centre

Nine weeks rotating through phlebotomy, haematology, microbiology and chemical pathology. Ran PSA, anti-CCP and troponin I on i-Chroma, HbA1c on Clover, and rapid kit tests for Chlamydia, H. pylori and faecal occult blood.
